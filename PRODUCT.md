# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People learning a hard concept (the author first, and anyone he shares a link with). They arrive from a chat or a link, usually curious about one specific idea, and want to manipulate it rather than read about it. Context is a browser tab, often mobile, attention is short.

## Product Purpose

A gallery of "explorable explanations": self-contained interactive artifacts (often something Claude sketched while teaching) rebuilt so you can poke at them. The shell exists to get out of the way, make a piece findable, and frame it well. Success is when someone opens an artifact, plays with it, and understands the idea faster than prose would have let them.

## Brand Personality

Editorial, quiet, exact. A field notebook kept by someone who cares about craft, not a SaaS product or a flashy portfolio. Warm paper and ink rather than dark-mode neon. The shell is confident enough to be plain; the artifacts carry the color and motion.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages (gradient hero, feature-card grid, hero-metric template).
- Dark-mode developer-tool aesthetic (terminal green, neon on near-black).
- Busy dashboards or anything that competes with the artifact for attention.
- AI-default cream-and-eyebrow scaffolding (tracked uppercase kicker over every section).

## Design Principles

- The shell serves the artifact. Chrome stays quiet so the embedded piece is the loudest thing on screen.
- Editorial over decorative. Hierarchy comes from type scale, weight, and whitespace, not boxes and accents.
- Motion clarifies arrival and state, never performs. Everything has a reduced-motion equivalent.
- Findable in one glance. A visitor should know what a piece teaches from its card alone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Visible keyboard focus on every interactive element, body and label text at or above 4.5:1, full `prefers-reduced-motion` support, and touch targets at 44px on coarse pointers.
